Atelier

Six lanes of a pool

Every job moves in its own lane; at the survey we mark together which lanes your pool needs.

  1. 1
    straighten

    Survey and measuring

    Cracks, grout and coping stones are photographed before draining; you get a written survey report.

    pool
  2. 2
    texture

    Stone coping

    Natural stone coping with a non-slip finish; joints are closed with a flexible, water-resistant filler.

    pool
  3. 3
    grid_view

    Mosaic and lining

    Loose mosaic is removed, the surface sealed and re-laid; colour samples are tried under pool water.

    pool
  4. 4
    settings

    Plant room

    The pump, sand filter and control panel are checked; an energy-efficient pump is quoted if needed.

    pool
  5. 5
    water_drop

    Water balance

    pH, chlorine and alkalinity are read at every visit and logged; dosing follows the reading.

    pool
  6. 6
    ac_unit

    Closing the season

    Before winter the water level is lowered, lines are drained and the cover is fitted; the spring opening is booked.

    pool
